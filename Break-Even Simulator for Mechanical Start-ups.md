# Break-Even Simulator

Find how many units a mechanical start-up must sell to cover all costs.

## Inputs

Fixed costs per period (rent, salaries, machines) Selling price per unit Variable cost per unit (material, labour, power) Expected sales (units) Target profit (optional)

## Results

## What-if: change selling price

Break-even units = Fixed cost ÷ (Price − Variable cost). Educational tool; figures depend on your inputs.
